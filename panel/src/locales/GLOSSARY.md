`src-tauri/locales/app.yml`. StarCraft and EUD terms follow the English names
SCMDraft 2 and EUD Editor use. Product names (euddraft, epScript, eudplib,
SCMDraft 2, EUD Editor, Tailscale, StarCraft) are never translated.
